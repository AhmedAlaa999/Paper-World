# Paper World 
Paper World is a 2D platformer game where your mission is to explore the map and collect all **13 keys** before time runs out. You have 60 seconds on the countdown timer, so move quickly, explore carefully, and find every key to win!

## Gameplay
Explore the world, navigate the map, and collect all 13 keys to complete your mission. Keep an eye on the countdown timer because you have limited time to find every key.

## How to Play
- Move around the map and explore your surroundings.
- Collect all **13 keys** scattered throughout the level.
- You have **60 seconds** to complete your mission.
- If you collect all the keys before time runs out, **you win!**
- If the timer reaches 0 before you collect all the keys, **you lose!**

## Mission
Your mission is simple: find all 13 keys before the countdown reaches zero. Can you explore the world, collect every key, and complete your mission in time?

## Features
- **Key collection system** with a live key counter.
- **Countdown timer** with a time limit.
- **Win and lose conditions** based on your progress.
- **2D platformer gameplay** with player movement and animations.
- **Sound effects** when collecting keys.
- **Camera following** the player.
- **Custom UI** with a timer, key counter, and result messages.

## Built With
- Godot Engine
- GDScript
