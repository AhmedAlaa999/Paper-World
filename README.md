# Paper World

**Paper World** is a 2D platformer with endless-runner mechanics where your goal is to collect the key while staying ahead of an automatically accelerating camera. As the camera gets faster, collect apples to boost your movement speed and survive for as long as you can!

## Gameplay

Run, jump, and collect items while keeping up with the camera. The challenge gets harder as the camera gradually accelerates, so every apple can make a difference between winning and losing.

## How to Play

- Collect the **key** to win the game.
- Collect **apples** to increase your movement speed.
- Stay ahead of the camera as it gradually gets faster.
- Watch the countdown timer and make every second count.
- If the camera passes you or time runs out, **you lose!**

## Mission

Collect the key, use apples to boost your speed, and stay ahead of the accelerating camera. Can you reach your goal before the camera catches up with you?

## Features

- **Key collection system** with a live counter.
- **Apple speed boosts** that increase the player's movement speed.
- **Automatically moving camera** that gradually accelerates.
- **READY! → GO! intro** before gameplay begins.
- **Countdown timer** with a time limit.
- **Win and lose systems** with frozen gameplay when the game ends.
- **2D platformer movement** with jumping and animations.
- **Sound effects** and custom UI elements.

## Built With
